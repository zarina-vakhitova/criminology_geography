What the dotted line means

A region on the line holds the same share of criminology authorship as it holds of the world economy. Above it, the region is over-represented in the discipline; below it, under-represented.

Why the Anglosphere falls

Part of this decline is real and part is an artefact of coverage: Web of Science added many non-core journals over the period. Within the eleven journals indexed continuously since 2000, the Anglosphere share falls only from 95% to 85%.

Sources

Criminology shares: 38,746 articles in 74 Criminology & Penology journals, by corresponding-author country. Economic shares: World Bank World Development Indicators, GDP at purchasing power parity. The four regions shown do not exhaust the world.
